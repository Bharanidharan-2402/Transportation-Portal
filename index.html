Frequently Asked Questions

Navigate to the "Apply for Pass" section, fill in the 4-step form with your personal details, route, and documents. Once submitted, you'll receive a reference number. The pass will be processed within 3 working days. Your application is stored securely.
Morning buses depart from the first stop between 6:15 AM – 8:00 AM and arrive at college by 8:15 – 8:30 AM. Evening departure from college is at 4:30 PM. Check the Routes section for exact timings per stop. Long-distance routes (Villupuram, Kumbakonam) start earlier.
Yes, route changes can be requested at the Transport Office (Room G-02, Admin Block). Submit a written request with your Student ID and preferred new route. Changes are subject to seat availability and take effect within 5 working days.
Report the loss immediately. A duplicate can be issued for ₹200. Submit a written application with a copy of your Student ID. The duplicate will be issued within 2 working days. You can also check your pass details on this portal's Dashboard.
AC buses currently operate on Routes 2 (Cuddalore), 6 (Sirkazhi), and 7 (Villupuram). AC pass pricing is approximately 40% higher. We plan to expand AC services to all routes by next academic year. Use the Route Comparison tool to check options.
Refunds are available within the first 15 days of the semester. After that, a pro-rata refund (minus 15% admin charge) is processed only for medical reasons with proper documentation. Contact the Accounts section.
